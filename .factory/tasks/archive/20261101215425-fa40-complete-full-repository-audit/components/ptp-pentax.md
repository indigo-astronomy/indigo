## Candidate PP-01 — Full Pentax registry leaks the rejected device and USB reference

Functional/resource ownership, low severity, high confidence, nonblocking audit follow-up; owner coordinator. `indigo_drivers/ccd_pentax/indigo_ccd_pentax.c:1684–1704` allocates private state, calls libusb_ref_device and allocates device before searching ten registry slots. If all slots are occupied the loop never attaches it and no cleanup follows: both allocations and reference are unreachable, and unplug/shutdown only traverse registered slots. Repeated arrival at capacity therefore leaks even though the driver remains alive. Existing unplug private-data repair handles registered devices and does not cover this branch. Minimal fix: reserve/find slot before allocating/ref, or free/unref the unregistered attempt when no slot exists; reject duplicate pending arrivals consistently. Add fake registry-full plus recovery/ownership regression. No11-camera or USB runtime test was performed; source ownership proof is sufficient, hardware access unnecessary. No matching active record was found.
