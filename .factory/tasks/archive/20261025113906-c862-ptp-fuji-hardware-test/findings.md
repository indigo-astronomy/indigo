# Findings

## Sony ILCE-7RM4: capture lost after a reconnect, exposure stays BUSY
Observed 2026-10-25 on 3.0.0.58 with the Raspberry Pi 5 (owner requested an extra Sony run before switching the camera off). Recorded run `python3 tools/run_driver_test.py ccd_ptp --hw --device "Sony Alpha A7RIV"` at 13:00 UTC: 15/13, `Reconnect` and the following `Shutdown and reinit` failed; the same code passed `Reconnect` at 12:46 (that run failed only on a suite restore defect fixed afterwards). Two further diagnostic runs failed the same way; one run (12:48) also saw the camera re-enumerate on USB (dmesg, 12:56:45). A bus trace of the third reconnect iteration shows CCD_EXPOSURE going BUSY right after CONNECTION OK and no PTP event from the camera afterwards (no ObjectAdded, no error), and the exposure stays BUSY until the client gives up after 60 s. Three manual disconnect/connect/expose cycles through indigo_server did not reproduce it. Not fixed here: the camera had to be switched off; the cause (camera state after repeated sessions in MF, or a missing capture completion deadline in `ptp_sony_exposure()`) is unknown. Next: a capture completion deadline that ends the exposure ALERT, a fake case for a camera that never reports the object, and a hardware rerun of `Reconnect`.
