# Catalogue licence review

Not legal advice. Reviewed 2026-11-05. The CDS site refused connections from this host, so the CDS pages were read from Internet Archive 2025 snapshots.

## Terms found
- VizieR rules of usage: data are "free of usage in a scientific context"; original authors and publication references including the publisher have to be cited; commercial usage "is subject to rules depending of the origin"; the ReadMe of a catalogue states a copyright if one exists.
- CDS general terms: re-use must comply with the licence of each dataset; datasets with only public information are distributed under an Open Licence, ODbL or CC-BY; a specific licence is shown on the dataset page.
- INDIGO licence: BSD-style, no restriction on commercial use.

## Conclusion
No statement forbids this use, and none clearly permits it. The citation requirement is met in the source comment. Open points for the owner:
1. INDIGO allows commercial redistribution, while the VizieR rules grant free use only in a scientific context and leave commercial use to the rules of the origin.
2. The licence of SIMBAD data was not confirmed. The product table takes Abell, Collinder and Melotte sizes and positions from it, plus van den Bergh positions.
3. Written confirmation from CDS (cds-question@unistra.fr, address from memory) would settle points 1 and 2 before publication.

The owner should decide on these before integration or publication.
