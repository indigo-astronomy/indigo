# Specification

AC-001: Identify both serial devices and establish exclusive access.
AC-002: Compare letter and numeric direction encodings using bounded ST4 pulses and independent NYX coordinate observations, with idle baseline and a direct mount guide positive control where needed.
AC-003: Record actual commands, measurements, limitations and cleanup; recommend only the behavior supported by observed mount movement.

Complexity: high, because physical guide response and coordinate reporting may differ and no serial acknowledgement distinguishes dialects. Risk: medium, bounded physical mount motion. No slews, homing or firmware updates.
