# Guider Agent refactoring and validation record

Status: hardware-free validation complete. Production defects `DRV-124` through `DRV-133` and `DRV-182` are fixed and focused-tested. Hardware validation was not performed.

## Current-state audit

- The Guider Agent is a hand-written agent/filter driver tested through the public INDIGO bus with the production agent, filter and CCD simulator. The test boundary keeps real agent timers, image analysis, correction algorithms and configuration paths, with a test-local configuration directory.
- `integration/test_agent_guider.c` contains fork-isolated cases. Each child starts INDIGO independently, owns temporary files/logs and performs cleanup; the parent removes child artifacts even after assertion failures, signals or watchdog timeouts.
- Normal preview images come from the CCD simulator. A deterministic camera boundary supplies Gaussian-star or full-frame centroid fixtures, and a guider spy records pulse direction and duration, translating software pulse commands into image displacement at 10 px/s. This is software boundary evidence, not physical relay timing or real mount motion.
- Covered behavior includes metadata and public properties, missing peer preconditions, one-shot and continuous preview, RAW/FITS restoration, retries, camera disconnection/reselection, star selection, detection/correction modes, pulse direction and thresholds, DEC modes, calibration, loss-of-star policy, dithering, PPEC, Multi Kernel GP (own settings, statistics, reset and learned model, kept across switches between PPEC and MKGP), logging, configuration reload, additional instances, related-mount coordination and active shutdown.
- Remaining gaps include physical mount/camera timing, long-period PPEC prediction/convergence, full-duration timeout paths, forced allocation/attach failure, real network/BLOB download transport, serial/USB behavior of underlying drivers and Linux/Windows execution.

## Acceptance boundary

- DRV-130 is an accepted known failure awaiting manual approval. Keep `dither RA projection preserves magnitude` as a regression that records the issue, but do not change the RA-only dithering implementation merely because this case fails during future Guider Agent testing or refactoring.
- The test suite validates the Guider Agent's public bus behavior, correction logic, process state, fake camera boundary and software pulse handling. It does not validate physical ST4/electrical relay accuracy, real mount response, physical camera timing, or manufacturer-specific serial/USB guide-port behavior.
- Sanitizer results instrument the test, agent, filter and selected framework/configuration boundary used by the test. They do not claim whole-program instrumentation of prebuilt simulator or library archives.
- Hardware tests were not run. No hardware validation is claimed.

## Linux agent test run (2026-09-24)

- Environment: Linux x86_64 (Ubuntu, GNU ld 2.42), simulator only, strict bus locking always on (the framework's `indigo_use_strict_locking` switch was removed in the same session).
- `dither RA projection preserves magnitude` passes: DRV-130 is closed as fixed in `indigo_drivers/REVIEW.md`, so the accepted-failure note above is historical.
- `selection subframe restore` failed deterministically because the CCD simulator never published the sensor size set through `SIMULATION_SETUP` (see `ccd_simulator/REFACTOR.md`, 2026-09-24). Fixed in the simulator; no Guider Agent change.
- `dither strategies` hung in about one of ten runs (SIGALRM). A thread dump showed a test-harness deadlock: the fake camera called `indigo_cancel_timer_sync()` from `change_property` with the bus locked while its `synthetic_frame()` timer waited for the bus lock in `indigo_update_property()`. The fake camera now aborts on its device queue, as `DRIVER_DEVELOPMENT_BASICS.md` requires; the case then passed 30 of 30 repetitions.
- Cooperation with the production Imager and Mount agents (dithering handshake, aborts while dithering, Mount Agent `ABORT_RELATED_PROCESS` on slew and park) is covered by `integration/test_agent_imager_guider_mount.c`, see `agent_imager/REFACTOR.md`.
- Result: 88 / 88 simulated cases passed; hardware tests 0 / 0.

## Calibration step (2026-09-29, 2026-09-30)

- `AGENT_GUIDER_SETTINGS.STEP0` = 0 means auto, and 0 is the minimum and default. Auto estimates the step from the pixel scale in `CCD_LENS_FOV` at an assumed 0.5x sidereal guide rate, or sets 0.2 s without a pixel scale, and writes it to `STEP0`. The step is saved with the calibration, so only the first calibration estimates it; from then on it behaves like an entered step.
- One step serves backlash clearing and the calibration legs whenever the settings allow it. A calibration leg aims at `MIN_CALIBRATION_DRIFT` in 0.4 x `MAX_CALIBRATION_STEPS` pulses (8 of the default 20). Backlash clearing needs more than `MIN_BL_DRIFT` in `MAX_BL_STEPS` pulses (20 times as many on RA only); when 1.2 times that per pulse still leaves a leg 0.25 x `MAX_CALIBRATION_STEPS` pulses (5), the step aims at it instead, if larger. With 2 px of drift and the default backlash settings that is 0.36 px per pulse, 2 px in 5.6 pulses; with the default 5 px it changes nothing, and with 1 px, where no step suits both, the step suits the legs alone.
- The step, estimated or entered, is checked once per calibration. After Dec backlash clearing (RA without Dec calibration) one more pulse on the same axis measures how fast the star moves. The step is kept when its drift per pulse takes a leg 0.2 to 0.8 x `MAX_CALIBRATION_STEPS` pulses and, where backlash counts, clears backlash; otherwise it is set to the target above, within 0.05 to 2 s. A step the agent sets always passes its own check. The agent reports the speed and whether the step fit or what it changed to.
- Backlash clearing pulses from one reference frame until the star drifts `MIN_BL_DRIFT`. A round of `MAX_BL_STEPS` pulses that falls short lengthens the pulse, starting from the step, by 1.5 times the move still missing over the move of the round, at least 1.5 and at most 8 times and at most 2 s, and the pulses go on from the same reference, so the drift made counts. A measuring pulse that moves the star less than 0.2 px is lengthened the same way and backlash clearing retried. RA backlash clearing keeps the pulse that moved the mount on Dec. The cap keeps a single pulse from moving the star out of its search area once a fast mount starts moving; longer pulses rather than more of them also move mounts that ignore very short pulses. Backlash clearing never changes the step itself.
- A reported calibration (`MIN_CALIBRATION_DRIFT` 2 px, `MAX_CALIBRATION_STEPS` 20, simulator at 12.1"/px, 0.56 px/s) showed why: the 8 pulse step, 0.401 s, moved the star 2.5 of 3 px in its first backlash round, the lengthened second round started again from a new reference frame and took another 8 s, and every calibration repeated it because the step fit the legs. Now the step is 0.36 px per pulse and clears backlash in the first round, and a round that still falls short continues from the drift it made.
- A star that does not move with 2 s pulses fails calibration with "The star does not move with 2 s pulses, check that the selected guider drives this mount". That is how a wrong or unwired guider shows: its pulses are accepted and nothing moves. With the defaults it takes three backlash clearing rounds of 10 pulses, 0.2, 1.6 and 2 s, about 38 s of pulses; failing sooner would also fail mounts with large Dec backlash.
- The calibration legs never halve or double the step and calibration never restarts for it: the Dec leg fails with "Drift is too slow" when `MAX_CALIBRATION_STEPS` pulses do not reach the drift, which after the check means the mount stopped moving. `change_step()` and its "Drift is too fast" check are gone.
- This replaces the first version (`0x03000033`), which estimated the step in advance and let the legs halve and double it. With `MIN_CALIBRATION_DRIFT` 1 px and the default backlash settings no step suited both backlash clearing (more than 0.3 px per pulse) and the Dec leg (at most 0.25 px per pulse, no fewer than 4 pulses): backlash clearing doubled the step, the Dec leg halved it, and calibration alternated without end, as it did with such an entered step before.
- Known limit: a mount that ignores pulses shorter than a threshold can be measured with a pulse above the threshold and given a step below it. Calibration then fails in the Dec leg instead of looping; the old algorithm failed on such a mount as well.
- `model_camera()` enters 0.2 s. Changed cases: `calibration adaptive minimum` (a 40 px/s mount completes at the 0.05 s floor; at 10 px/s an entered 0.5 s, 2 pulses, is changed to 0.125 s, 8 pulses), `calibration adaptive maximum` (the frozen mount fails at a 2 s clearing pulse with the entered 1.5 s kept), `calibration no motion` (also expects the guider message and 3 clearing rounds, 0.2, 1.6 and 2 s), `calibration single pulse speed` and `calibration directional speed` (one more RA pulse, the measuring one). The model can ignore pulses shorter than `minimum_moving_pulse`, and the test client records the agent's messages.
- New cases, each checking the step against the pulses aimed at and the Dec speed calibration measured:
  - `calibration step estimate`: auto at 50 cm, 2.15"/px, with the model at 0.5x sidereal (3.5 px/s) and 2 px of drift; the estimate, 0.103 s (5.6 pulses), fits, is kept and clears backlash without lengthening the pulse.
  - `calibration step small drift`: auto at 20 cm, 5.36"/px, 1.4 px/s and 1 px of drift; backlash clearing, 1.3 of 3 px, lengthens its pulse once to 0.186 s and goes on, and the estimate, 0.089 s (8 pulses), is kept. The first version alternated without end here.
  - `calibration step saved short for backlash`: the reported case at 1.4 px/s; the saved 8 pulse step, 0.178 s, moves the star 2.5 of 3 px in the first backlash round, clearing goes on with 0.267 s pulses and the step is changed to 0.265 s; the next calibration keeps it and clears backlash in the first round.
  - `calibration step entered and default`: at 1.12 px/s an entered 0.3 s fits and is kept; auto without a focal length starts at 0.2 s, backlash clearing lengthens its pulse to 0.3 s and goes on, and the step is changed to 0.317 s.
  - `calibration step ignored pulses`: a mount ignoring pulses under 150 ms is measured with 0.2 s, given 0.0625 s and fails in the Dec leg.
- The web UI's calibration step presets replace 0.01, below the former minimum, with 0 and name it auto in the tooltip.
